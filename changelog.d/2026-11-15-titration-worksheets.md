An administrator can select an alkalinity (to pH 4.5, or low alkalinity with two endpoints) or EDTA total hardness calculation for a test. Analysts then enter the burette readings, sample volume and titrant normality, and the result in mg/L as CaCO3 is worked out and kept with its worksheet. A correction keeps the earlier worksheet.

## Technical notes

Alkalinity also keeps the measured endpoint pH. The equations follow EPA 310.1 and EPA 130.2 as historical sources; this does not claim current regulatory approval.
