The Spreadsheet import page offers starter files to check, edit and import: method versions, analytes, tests, holding-time and preservation rules, and fortified blank and matrix recovery limits for EPA 200.7, 200.8, 245.1 and 300.0, each quoting its public source. They are starting points, not laboratory policy; check every row against your own methods, permits and current rules.

## Technical notes

The files carry no reporting limits, soil tests or preparation methods.
