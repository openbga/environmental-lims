title: Download issued results as an Excel workbook
area: Reports

Clients can download current and earlier report versions as Excel workbooks. They can reuse their CSV column template to choose which fields appear and how they are labeled and ordered. The workbook keeps sample identifiers and reported values as text, including leading and trailing zeros.

## Technical notes

Excel downloads use XlsxWriter, included in the locked application dependencies.
