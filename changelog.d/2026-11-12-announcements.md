Administrators can post notices under Setup, Announcements. They appear on the sign-in page, the staff dashboard and the client portal home page, with an optional end time. Withdrawing a notice is permanent, and posting, changing and withdrawing are recorded in the history.

## Technical notes

Adds migration 0004 in core (the announcement table).
